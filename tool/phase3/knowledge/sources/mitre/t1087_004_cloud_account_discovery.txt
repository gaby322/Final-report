# actions: GenerateCredentialReport,GetCredentialReport

Technique: T1087.004 — Account Discovery: Cloud Account
Tactic: Discovery
Platforms: IaaS, SaaS

Source: MITRE ATT&CK
URL: https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1087/004/

Description:
Adversaries may attempt to enumerate cloud accounts to understand the identity structure of a target environment. MITRE notes that cloud APIs may be used to retrieve information about accounts, users, roles, and credentials in order to identify valuable targets and understand account posture.

AWS examples cited by MITRE on the T1087.004 technique page:
- aws iam list-users
- aws iam list-roles

Framework-curated AWS interpretation (NOT cited on the MITRE page):
- GenerateCredentialReport, GetCredentialReport are framework-selected
  credential-enumeration actions treated by this project as signals under
  T1087.004 because they return principal and credential inventory. These
  actions are NOT listed as MITRE-cited examples on the T1087.004 page; any
  paper reference must present them as framework-curated, not MITRE-supported.

Detection:
Cloud account discovery may be visible through IAM and identity enumeration activity, especially when credential reporting or identity inventory actions are performed by principals that do not normally require them.

Mitigation:
Defensive value comes from restricting unnecessary identity enumeration permissions and monitoring unusual use of account inventory or credential-reporting capabilities.
