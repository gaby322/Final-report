# actions: CreatePolicyVersion,AttachUserPolicy

Technique: T1098.003 — Account Manipulation: Additional Cloud Roles
Tactics: Persistence, Privilege Escalation
Platforms: IaaS, Identity Provider, Office Suite, SaaS

Source: MITRE ATT&CK
URL: https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1098/003/

Description:
Adversaries may add additional roles or permissions to an adversary-controlled cloud account to maintain persistent access to a tenant. This account modification may follow account creation or other malicious account activity, and may also involve modifying existing valid accounts that the adversary has already compromised.

AWS examples explicitly noted by MITRE:
- CreatePolicyVersion to define a new version of an IAM policy
- AttachUserPolicy to attach an IAM policy with additional or distinct permissions to a compromised user account

Detection:
MITRE’s detection guidance focuses on identifying new IAM roles or policies attached to a user or service outside normal patterns or hours, and on behavioral chains in which a user is granted elevated privileges or roles following suspicious login or account creation activity.

Mitigation:
MITRE mitigation guidance includes:
- using multi-factor authentication for user and privileged accounts
- ensuring that all accounts use the least privileges they require
- ensuring that low-privileged accounts do not have permissions to add permissions to accounts or update IAM policies

Framework note:
In this framework, additional IAM policy- and role-manipulation actions may also be analyzed in Phase 2 and Phase 3 as researcher-defined implementation mappings. Those broader action mappings should not be presented as if they were all explicitly enumerated by MITRE under this technique.
